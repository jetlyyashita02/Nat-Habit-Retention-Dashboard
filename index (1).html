📖 Definitions & SKU decoder

Filters: Category / Variant / From–To month apply to each customer's first (entry) order (cohort semantics). Journey depth = total orders. Region = city mapped to North / West / South / East / Rest of India.

Seasonality tiles: orders counted by the variant's intended season line; Peak Season = largest bucket. Purchase timing: orders by calendar month — Cold Winter Dec–Feb · Hot Dry Mar–May · Hot Humid Jun–Sep · Post-Monsoon Oct–Nov.

V2V Loyalty % — customers with ≥2 orders whose 2nd order repeats an entry variant; V2C — 2nd order stays in an entry category. Avg Purchase Days = mean order1→order2 days within those groups; Avg Order Count = mean total orders of those groups.

Seasonality strength: Highly ≥40% of orders in peak timing season · Moderately 30–40% · Evergreen <30%. Grammage: Upsized = larger size · Lateral = same size other category · Downsized = smaller.

SKU code FC-<LINE>-<VARIANT>-<SIZE> — e.g. FC-HDDM-FB-050 = Face Malai · Hot Dry · Flax Bakuchi · 50g. Lines: CWDM Cold Winter · HDDM Hot Dry · HHDM Hot Humid · DM non-seasonal concern · AC Active Gel · HG Aloe Vera Gel. Multi-item orders are analysed on every item for tiles/loyalty and on the first SKU for transitions/conclusions.

Privacy: the file-picker reads your CSV entirely inside your browser — nothing is uploaded to any server.
